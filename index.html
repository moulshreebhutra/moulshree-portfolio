About

From pixels to product.

I studied computer science, then fell for product design and taught myself the craft. I started in design, with internships at Find Firms and Crux Intelligence, freelance work on Contra, and a role at LeadSquared where I designed the My Trips module and a mobile design system for a field sales app.

In April 2024 I joined Vudio.ai as a founding APM, working directly with the founder across product, customer success and design. We took Vudio from a video commerce tool to a full-stack marketing suite for D2C brands. I owned PLG and funnel optimisation, contributed to four zero-to-one launches (AI Studio, Content Library, Social Media Posting and DM Automation), and hired and led a pod of three designers.

I like the unglamorous parts of a product: onboarding, activation, the dashboard a customer opens late at night to decide whether to renew. I build things so the proof is visible, in numbers a merchant can see.

Now I am looking for a product team where I can keep owning problems end to end, alongside people who push my thinking.
